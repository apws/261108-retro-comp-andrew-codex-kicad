= Screw terminal holes facing out
:toc:
:toclevels: 2

`J5` was turned 180 degrees the wrong way. The wire holes faced into
the board, and the closed back of the Phoenix block sat on the right
edge. KiCad was closed, then the footprint was turned and saved.


== Which way the holes face

On `TerminalBlock_Phoenix_MKDS-1,5-2-5.08_1x02_P5.08mm_Horizontal` the
wire face is the footprint's +Y side. The STEP model ends at local
y = +5.2 mm. In this pcbnew a positive angle sends that +Y side toward
board +X, so +90 degrees sends the holes off the right edge.

The earlier placement used −90 degrees and called the opening the −Y
side. That put the holes toward the PIO headers.


== Where it sits

Pin 1 is at (156.8, 19.08) mm, angle +90. Pin 1 is still `+5V` and pin
2 is still `GND`. Because the part turned around, pin 1 is now the
lower pad and pin 2 is the upper one.

The hole face is at x = 162 mm, 2 mm past the outline. The pads stay
on the board: both centers are at x = 156.8 mm, so the copper ends
about 1.9 mm inside the edge. The courtyard runs from x = 151.0 mm to
x = 161.9 mm and from y = 10.9 mm to y = 22.2 mm. It does not meet the
corner hole `H2` or the header `J4`. Nothing else was moved. There are
still no signal tracks.


== Opening it

Open the project fresh. The file on disk has this turn. The 3D view
should show the two wire holes past the right edge.
