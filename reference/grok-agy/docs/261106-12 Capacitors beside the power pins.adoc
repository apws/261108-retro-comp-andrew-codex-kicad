= Capacitors beside the power pins
:toc:
:toclevels: 2

The 100 nF discs were in one row under the chips, and the 1 µF
radials were in one row along the top. KiCad was closed. Only the
capacitors moved. The reset capacitor `C1` stayed with `R1` and the
switch, because it is the reset timer, from `RESET_RC` to `GND`.


== What the schematic actually ties

`C2` to `C11` are all straight from `+5V` to `GND`. The sheet draws
them in one place and says one per package. There are ten packages:
the eight chips and the two oscillators. Nothing in the netlist pins
`C2` to `U1` by a private wire. The assignment below is the placement.

The 1 µF parts are not a shared bank. `C20` is across the MAX232 `C1`
pins, `C21` across `C2`, `C23` from `VS+` to `GND`, and `C24` from
`GND` to `VS-` (the positive end of that capacitor is ground). `C22`
is the only 1 µF from `+5V` straight to `GND`.

The discs are rotation 0. Pin 1 is `+5V`. Each one is outside the
package courtyard, on the side nearest that package's supply pin.

`C20`, `C21`, `C23` and `C24` are on the left side and just above `U6`,
next to the pump pins. The nearest pad is 4 to 6 mm from its pin.
`C22` is immediately left of the screw terminal.

Courtyards do not overlap. Pad copper stays inside the outline. The
inner planes were refilled. There are still no signal tracks.


== What this does not fix

A 5 mm disc does not fit in the 1.2 mm gap between the DIP bodies.
`U1` and `U4` therefore still have their bypass at the near end of a
long package, not beside the supply pin. On this four-layer board the
inner planes carry `+5V` and `GND`, so the pin and the capacitor both
meet a plane. The short loop the bypass wants is still the one beside
the pin. Opening the row, or folding the chips into two rows, is a
different placement. It was not done.
