= MAX232 beside the DE9
:toc:
:toclevels: 2

The MAX232 sat in the chip row, and `U4` ended 0.6 mm from the right
edge. KiCad was closed before this file was written.


== The move

`U6` and its capacitors moved left as a group. The pump capacitors
`C20`, `C21` and `C24` now start at x = 12.1 mm, about 2 mm clear of
the DE9 shell. `C23` is above that column, clear of `R2`. `C7` stayed
above pin 16.

The other seven DIP packages moved left by 9.5 mm, and their 100 nF
capacitors moved with them. `U4` now ends at x = 149.9 mm, so about
10 mm of the board is free on the right. The gap between `U6` and `U5`
is 8.2 mm.

`Y2` and `C11` moved with `U5`. Leaving the oscillator where it was
would have parked it over `U1` and pushed `C6` into it.

The DE9, the screw terminal, the headers, the holes, the reset parts
and `Y1` stayed put. Courtyards do not overlap. There are still no
signal tracks.
