== Authorized work

Falken requested implementation after the source and review were clarified. Clean up our own human-edited A3 drawing, preserve its placement, and create a separate `schematic-7805` variant with a protected nominal 9 V input and regulated +5 V supply. Then reroute the historical FirstZ80 board three ways: Manhattan, octilinear and FreeRouting. Preserve all completed earlier boards.

== Two circuits, explicitly separated

Our own schematic is based on Thomas Scherrer's GIF. It has no 7805 in its original form. Its PSU extension will retain our own schematic arrangement and the reviewed serial/reset connections. Mark's feedback is preserved in link:261109-2306-REVIEW-Mark-schematic-readability.adoc[the readability review].

The Reddit PCB is Andrew Lynch's expanded FirstZ80, recovered from https://github.com/lynchaj/duodyne/tree/66a5a63fd8338d12c115bc2bee5dff2f435afae4/%21Development/FirstZ80[commit 66a5a63, January 2024] through the locally saved Reddit printout. Its schematic already includes a 7805. The source PCB render matches Falken's saved WEBP. Its original footprint origins and rotations can therefore be read from native KiCad data rather than estimated from pixels.

FirstZ80 has 63 footprints on a 160 x 100 mm two-layer board, with no copper zones. The outline centre lines run from (60,60) to (220,160) mm; the 160.1 x 100.1 mm stroked bounding box is not the nominal board size. The main DIP columns are CPU, EEPROM, RAM, PPI and UART; supply parts occupy the upper left, serial connectors the upper right, the PPI connector the lower right, and clocks/decode/reset the lower area. This is a different placement style from our horizontal DIP arrangement: orderly component grouping is established first, and the wires must accommodate it.

== Differences to retain and explain

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Area |Historical FirstZ80 differences
|Power |LM7805 U9, series 1N5819 D10, barrel input J1, parallel screw-terminal J4, input/output reservoir capacitors and test points. Nominal 9 V input; thermal capability is not established by the board layout.
|PPI connector |One right-angle 2x13 IDC connector P1 carries all three 8-bit ports, rather than our three 1x8 headers. K1 selects GND or VCC for P1 pin 25; pin 26 is GND. The complete native pin mapping must be retained.
|Serial |Male DE9 with different mechanical geometry, RTS/CTS wiring, and an additional six-pin TTL connector. Our previous circuit uses a female DE9 and DTR/DSR signals.
|Memory |28C256 EEPROM instead of our 27C256 EPROM; pin 1 and pin 27 have different functions. Preserve the historical connection specification for the placement comparison and flag any functional limitations separately.
|Diagnostics |Nine LEDs and their resistors, different reset capacitance, and peripheral strap choices. No mounting holes in this snapshot, unlike our earlier board.
|Parts and references |LS gates, different oscillator footprints and reference numbering. Do not map nets by reference names from the earlier project.
|Presentation |The reference schematic uses a larger ANSI C sheet and short named bus connections; our wired experiment uses explicit A/D buses on A3. Learn readability principles without copying its drawing coordinates.
|Routing provenance |The source includes a Specctra/FreeRouting-style `.rules` file. The native tracks alone do not prove manual routing; describe the supplied board as the historical reference rather than claim it is hand-routed.
|Migration |Old autogenerated net names differ from KiCad 10 names. Compare net membership by (reference,pin) sets before renaming; do not treat mere naming changes as electrical errors. Preserve and report inherited warnings.
|===

== Implementation and validation

. Preserve the original source and the current human-edited schematic before any repair.
. Repair pin overshoots, text collisions and crowded labels locally. Retain all 301 original pin assignments in the drawing-only cleanup, run native ERC and the independent drawn-path/bus checker, then refresh previews.
. Add the PSU only in a separate schematic variant, with an explicit expected netlist delta. Check diode polarity, regulator pin numbering, capacitor polarity and separation of raw input from +5 V. Use the https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm7800.pdf[manufacturer's regulator data]. This is a design experiment, not a measured thermal or hardware qualification.
. Prepare a separate FirstZ80 project with portable snapshots of the embedded symbols/footprints. Audit source schematic/PCB connectivity, normalize only proven net-name changes, and freeze footprint positions, rotations, pad geometry, outline and rules.
. Remove all original tracks/vias and zones from the common routing checkpoint. Every method starts with that same cleared board; no source power or signal copper is imported.
. Route all connected nets on the two available copper layers, including GND and power. Use common signal/power widths, clearances, via dimensions and edge/drill protection. Record parameters and retries. If a method cannot complete, retain the failed evidence and continue resolving it without changing placement or silently weakening constraints.
. Run fresh native DRC, schematic parity and unconnected checks, then verify unchanged footprint/pad geometry and circuit membership. Keep inherited source warnings distinct from new routing violations.
. Publish separate ordinary KiCad projects, routing previews, hashes, metrics and a timestamped results note. Compare the three new results against each other; do not treat changed circuitry, placement and layer count as a controlled comparison against the earlier four-layer metrics.
